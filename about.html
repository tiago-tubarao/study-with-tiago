Why I Make These

I'm a BSN nursing student, not a company and not an instructor. I'm still in the middle of learning this material myself.

Some topics clicked fast. Other topics did not. Pharmacology lists, lab values, patho chains, nursing priorities - sometimes the information was technically there, but I still needed someone to say, this is what matters, this is how it shows up, and this is how I would think through it.

So I started explaining the material out loud like I was talking to another student at the table. Then I turned those explanations into videos, flashcards, My Notes, and study rooms so I could come back to them before exams.

That's the real reason this site exists. I wanted one place where the material felt less scattered: watch the topic, review the cards, read the notes, then test whether it actually stuck.

I'm not pretending to have it all figured out. I'm showing my work, sharing what helped me, and building the study room I wish I had when I was overwhelmed.

My Study Philosophy

1. Explain it like we are studying together.
If I can't say it in normal words, I probably do not understand it yet. The goal is not fancy language. The goal is a sentence you can actually use on an exam.

2. Make the important things visible.
Videos, cards, color, tables, and My Notes are there so the priority points do not get buried inside a giant wall of text.

3. Practice before confidence.
I do not want to just feel familiar with the material. I want to answer questions, miss things, see the rationale, and fix the gap before the exam.

4. Keep it honest.
This is student-to-student study support. I mark what is ready, what is still being built, and what you should verify with your instructor, textbook, or current guidelines.
