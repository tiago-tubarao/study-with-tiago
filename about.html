Why This Site Exists

Study with Tiago is built by a BSN nursing student for nursing students who need more than another wall of notes.

The site connects plain-language videos, visual flashcards, My Notes packets, and practice questions so a topic can move from explanation to recall.

Each topic is organized against standard nursing curricula, then turned into a study loop: watch, check coverage, drill cards, and test with rationales.

The goal is simple: make the next study session easier to start and easier to repeat.

Study Approach

Explain it simply.
Videos break topics down into plain language before the flashcards ask for recall.

Organize by topic.
Drug families, body systems, and final-review rooms are grouped so visitors do not need to know one class's exact exam labels.

Practice before comfort.
Confidence dots, flip cards, and NCLEX-style questions turn passive review into a check-yourself loop.

Keep it scannable.
Short sections, color cues, timestamps, and visual anchors make each page easier to return to during a study session.
